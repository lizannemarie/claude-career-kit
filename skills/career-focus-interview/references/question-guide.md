# Career-focus question guide

Use these as starting points, not a script. One question at a time.

| Topic | Sample opener | Follow-ups worth asking |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | "Think of a recent week at work that felt great. What were you doing?" | What specifically made it good? How often did that happen? |
| Drain | "What kind of work leaves you flat, even when it goes well?" | Is it the work, the people, or the pace? |
| Values | "What would make you turn down an otherwise great offer?" | Mission, autonomy, flexibility, stability, growth, compensation: rank the top three. |
| Environment | "Describe the team and company where you did your best work." | Size, stage, pace, how decisions get made, structure vs ambiguity. |
| Role targets | "Which job titles would you click on first in a list?" | Which would you never click? Adjacent roles you would consider? |
| Level | "What level are you targeting, and what's the floor you'd accept?" | People management: required, nice, or not wanted? |
| Location | "Remote, hybrid, onsite: what works, and where?" | Home metro and nearby cities that count; relocation yes/no; country for remote. |
| Compensation | "What base salary floor should the daily search use?" | Only applied when a posting lists pay. Currency. |
| Industries to avoid | "Any industries you won't work in?" | Hard no vs prefer-not. |
| Exclusions | "Any role categories that should never be surfaced, even if they match keywords?" | For example sales/account executive, recruiting, contract-only. |
| Timing | "What's your timeline, and is anything driving it?" | Only for context; not stored in config. |

End with: "Is there anything about what you want next that I haven't asked?"
