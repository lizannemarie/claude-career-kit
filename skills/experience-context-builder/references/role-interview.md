# Role-by-role interview prompts

Ask one at a time, and skip what the resume already answers clearly.

1. **Scope**: "In one sentence, what were you accountable for in this role?"
2. **Team**: "How big was your team, who reported to you (if anyone), and who did you report to?"
3. **Ownership**: "Which results did you own outright, and which did you contribute to as a partner?"
4. **Metrics**: "What numbers moved because of your work? How were they measured (A/B test, holdout, dashboard, finance report)?"
5. **Tools**: "Which tools or platforms did you use hands-on, and which did you only review or manage?"
6. **Projects**: "Which two or three projects would you most want an interviewer to ask about?"
7. **Stories**: for each project, ask for the full story (situation, what you diagnosed, what you decided, what happened, what lasted). Capture it verbatim-ish for the archive.
8. **Boundaries**: "Is there anything people might assume you did in this role that you didn't?"
9. **Dates and titles**: confirm exact titles and start/end months, and note any title changes.
